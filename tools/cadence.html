A small tool for songwriters. Your lyrics stay in this page and are gone when you close it.

Cadence

How many syllables is each line asking you to fit in the bar?

One line of the song per line. A blank line starts a new section. A line like [Verse] or Hook: names the section and is not counted.

Line by line

How to read it

The big number is the syllables in the line. The bar under the words is the time the line gets: the divisions are beats and each tick is one syllable. Ticks are spread evenly, which a real delivery never is, so treat the bar as how crowded the line is and not where the words land.

A line is flagged when it asks for more syllables a second than the limit you set, or when it is well out of step with the other lines in its section.

When the count is wrong

Tap any word to change how many syllables it counts as. Your count applies everywhere that word appears. Some words really do go both ways: fire, every, family. Sing it the way you sing it.

The counting is spelling rules first. Tested against a pronunciation dictionary, the rules alone were right for about 92 in 100 of the 30,000 most common English words. A table of about 2,400 exceptions corrects the rest of those. Rarer words, names and made-up words go by the rules alone.

What it does not do

It does not know where the stresses fall, and it does not check rhyme. It only reads English spelling. The rules and the page are in one script; the exceptions are derived from the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary.
