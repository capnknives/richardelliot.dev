Small tools. Each one runs in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.

Side doors

The projects are down the corridor. These are the smaller rooms off it.

Trace

Type a message and follow it through the assistant I run at home: which check routes it, what it costs in model calls, and which guard gets to stop it.

From WITS.

Forget me

Teach a small neural network one fact about you, then try to take it back two ways. The model trains in your browser, and you can watch every weight it changes.

The idea behind retractbench, small enough to hold.

Knobs

How AI works, with working parts. Read a rule a person wrote, watch a small network learn the same job from examples by turning 33 knobs, then try to find where it put the rule.

For anyone who suspects it is all smoke. Start here, then try Forget me.

Cadence

Paste lyrics and see how many syllables each line asks you to fit in a bar at your tempo. It flags the crowded lines, and you can hear any line as clicks.

Pool water balance

Five test readings in, a dosing plan out. Drag a reading and watch the pool wall pit or crust over.

Feeder match

Pick a snake and enter its weight. Get the feeder size, how often to feed, and how many to pick up for the month.

First version, built for a friend who breeds snakes.
