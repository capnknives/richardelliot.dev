Aquainspection

Pool inspection reports, spoken on site.

The demo is invitation only. The code is private.

The problem

A pool inspection means a technician with wet hands working down a long checklist, then typing it all up later. The typing is where detail gets lost and where reports get delayed.

What it does

  • The technician describes what they see, out loud, in their own words. The app turns that into a structured report.
  • Equipment can be dictated too: pumps, filters, heaters and everything else at the pad.
  • Reads serial numbers from equipment nameplates to work out how old each piece is.
  • Photos attach to the report or stay internal, as the company prefers.
  • Each company chooses which reports get generated from one inspection.

Designed for the field

Most technicians will use it from a phone, outdoors, so the phone is the first screen it is designed for. When voice mishears, there is always a quick way to type or photograph the detail instead.

How it got here

It started from one sentence about what a pool company wanted. The research behind it is mine: how comparable inspection software works, the rules that apply in Texas, how home inspections actually run, and how each manufacturer encodes dates in its serial numbers.
