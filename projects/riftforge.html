Riftforge

A text-game engine, and the world that runs on it.

Play in your browserEngine on GitHubGame site

The engine

Riftforge is a MUD engine: the server behind a multiplayer text game. It is written in pure Python on asyncio using only the standard library, with no frameworks and no third-party runtime dependencies.

It supplies the reusable parts of a text world, such as travel, combat, an economy, planes and the gates between them. A game's own lore and writing live in a separate project that pins a tagged release of the engine.

The game

Mortals and Monsters is the world built on it: ordinary American towns laid over a layered afterlife, played in a browser or over telnet. It is free, and it is in alpha.

  • Your character stays in the world when you log off.
  • Towns and dungeons sit on real geography, and you travel between them.
  • Non-player characters work, eat and get into trouble using the same commands players type.
  • A screen-reader mode flattens the framed interface, and nothing is signalled by colour alone.

Why it is here

A persistent world is a long-running system: its state has to survive restarts, disconnects and many people acting at once. The engine handles that on its own, without leaning on a framework.
