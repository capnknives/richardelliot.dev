The cellar

Where the site keeps how it was made.

What is down here

No framework and no build step. Every page is a plain HTML file, one stylesheet, and four small scripts you can read in the source.

  • The moving backdrops are one fragment shader with a mode for each room.
  • Titles travel between pages with the browser's own view transitions.
  • The workshop on the front page keeps eleven probes and two kinds of undo.
  • The front page is the inside of a box. Each visit collapses into a numbered world; the stars and the tint of the light are seeded from that number.
  • The typefaces are Sofia Sans, its extra-condensed cut, and Literata italic for the narrator, all served from this site.

Every command

look, examine, exits, go, back, inventory, help. Each room has things worth examining. One old magic word still does nothing.
