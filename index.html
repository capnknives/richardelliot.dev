About

I have read code for most of my life and write scripts as a hobby. For the last four years I have studied AI ethics and how models are made, with one stubborn focus: changing the model itself, in modular and inspectable ways, instead of wrapping it in more context.

I am studying AI software engineering, and I plan to continue on to a computer science degree. The projects here are how I practise: each one is a working system with real users or real measurements behind it.

The best place to reach me is GitHub, as capnknives.
