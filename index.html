    Why any of this.

    What I believe

    1. AI should send you exploring.

      A tool that only hands over answers makes people smaller. The good ones leave you more curious, and more capable, than they found you.

    2. Small, local and free beats big, central and controlled.

      Software should be able to run on your own machine, answer to you, and keep working when a company changes its mind.

    3. Forgetting is a feature we will need.

      If a model can be changed, people will ask for proof that a change can be reversed, and that their information is truly gone. Unlearning is how we live with AI with less fear, and I expect the law to require it.

    This site tries to practice the second one: no trackers, no cookies, no framework, fonts served from here, and every script short enough to read.

    Learned the slow way.

    Rules I work by

    • Keep the record.

      An undo is only exact if someone saved what was there before. Everything else is a good guess.

      From retractbench.

    • Run the control.

      Before blaming the edit, apply the same undo to a model nobody touched, and see what the undo does by itself.

      From retractbench.

    • The person stays the author.

      Software can suggest. It does not get to change someone's record of their own work without being asked.

      From StoryGecko.

    • Say what was not tested.

      Every change states what was checked and what was not. A gap you name is one somebody can close.

      From Aquainspection.
